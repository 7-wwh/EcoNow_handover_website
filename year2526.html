TERM 25/26

My 25/26 Term

My 25/26 planning for EcoNow can be explained in one word: Rebranding. This is why I plan to do this, and why this idea is controversial.

Section 01 — The Loop Ends Here

From the previous years, I felt like EcoNow is stuck in a loop, where we're making products that aren't attractive to the market. An RM30 candle that doesn't look very appealing or aesthetically pleasing — try comparing it to brands like Le Labo, Diptyque, Jo Malone, or even some local brands that you see in those premium night markets.

Because candles are a very different market, here's the usual customer profile of EcoNow:

Usual Customer Profile of EcoNow

  • • Looking for a unique scent that could be found (elsewhere)
  • • Using candles as part of their daily routine / ritual
  • • Looking for room fragrance
  • • Curious about candles and wish to try them out

A Realization

Realize that there's very little people that actually care about used cooking oil? Yes — if we're trying to make EcoNow into a premium product, or sell it at a premium market, this is not something that a customer would wish to know.

"Building an aesthetic candle with a unique scent profile."

So premium is always a necessity in the realm of scented candles — or maybe not the word "premium," but an eye-pleasing and soothing product. That's the direction I was trying to create.

Interactive Probe: Which Feeling Sells the Candle?

There's a branding event that I've attended where the speaker mentioned: "a customer didn't purchase because your product is built very good or something, they buy because it makes them feel good." Test the ideas below:

The Cooking Oil Analogy Imagine you're buying cooking oil from a grocery stall — why do you choose that brand? Cheap? It makes you feel like you bought a product that's worth it (feeling good). Healthy? You feel healthier after using that product (feeling good). So — what feeling are we going to initiate inside our customers? The feeling of helping society, or the good feeling of lighting a cute, nice candle in their bedroom, immersed in the scent they tried out at your booth? You choose.

Along with building an aesthetic candle, branding comes together — EcoNow's logo, font, everything doesn't even look aligned with a premium brand. Every part of this requires work as well, a lot of work. This 25/26 year we've also explored a different product category: air diffuser, perfume. We did have an external booth at TRX.

Section 03 — Product

There's Never a Product No One Will Buy

One thing that I learned about making products, when I was trying to start a 3D printing lamp shop (not yet established), is that there's never a product that no one will buy — only a product that's sold at the very wrong place.

If anyone approaches you and tells you that your candle is too expensive, you ask them why is it that they think it's expensive.

Diptyque can sell candles for RM300, a normal local brand "Moré" can sell it for RM139. So? Why can't you up the price of our candle to RM70, RM90?

The Most Important Part

You should deliver the value you sell. Premium products require premium branding — the feeling, as I've mentioned above.

Refining the Difference

One important thing to remember is that CANDLES ARE DIFFERENT FROM PERFUME. Perfume uses essential oil (the expensive ones), but for candles we use fragrance oil. Fragrance oil could be said to be artificial, because it's not organic — it burns more smoothly and the flame doesn't produce soot, a cleaner burn. That's why making perfume is much more different — I wouldn't suggest using fragrance oil to make perfume.

Temperature

Pour-Point Simulator

Candle is very sensitive to temperature. When we melt all the candles in a pot, we need to monitor the temperature closely.

We never add fragrance into the pot when we melt the wax — we add the fragrance as the very last step, waiting for the wax to cool down to around 80 degrees before the fragrance is poured in. Otherwise, due to the high heat of the pot, most of the fragrance is already lost into the atmosphere, which we don't want — we want it in the candle.

Section 06 — 3D Printer (A Controversial Idea)
A Controversial Idea

"Bear with me a little if you plan to sell this piece of machine."
