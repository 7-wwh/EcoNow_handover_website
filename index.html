01 — Where It Started

The History of EcoNow

If you think you already know this, read it anyway.

EcoNow began with one problem: children who are forced to leave the homes they've lived in for years, once they hit legal age, because there simply isn't enough space or resources to keep supporting them. EcoNow set out to tackle this by giving them real, hands-on entrepreneurial skills — something to actually stand on once they're out.

The whole thing started almost by accident — a sudden idea to recycle used cooking oil into candles, born during a summer summit. From there, the product line kept moving:

Candles Founding idea

Used cooking oil, repurposed into candles. The original concept EcoNow was built on.

EcoSoap Discontinued

An expansion into soap-making. Discontinued afterward — the working environment turned out to be highly acidic and difficult to sustain.

Mini Candles

A scaled-down product line, easier to produce and sell.

Air Diffuser, Perfume & New Scents My term

Under my term, R&D pushed further — trialing air diffusers, perfume, and new candle scents.

02 — Where It Stands Now

The Current State of EcoNow

This is the part you actually need to sit with.

EcoNow has lost its original focus. That's not just my opinion — it's Dr Roy's, and I agree with it. We've drifted toward profiting instead of educating and equipping the children with skills, which was the entire point of starting this in the first place.
