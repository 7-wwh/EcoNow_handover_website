01 — Where It Started

The History of EcoNow

If you think you already know this, read it anyway.

EcoNow began with one problem: children who are forced to leave the homes they've lived in for years, once they hit legal age, because there simply isn't enough space or resources to keep supporting them. EcoNow set out to tackle this by giving them real, hands-on entrepreneurial skills — something to actually stand on once they're out.

The whole thing started almost by accident — a sudden idea to recycle used cooking oil into candles, born during a summer summit. From there, the product line kept moving:

Candles Founding idea

Used cooking oil, repurposed into candles. The original concept EcoNow was built on.

EcoSoap Discontinued

An expansion into soap-making. Discontinued afterward — the working environment turned out to be highly acidic and difficult to sustain.

Mini Candles

A scaled-down product line, easier to produce and sell.

Air Diffuser, Perfume & New Scents My term

Under my term, R&D pushed further — trialing air diffusers, perfume, and new candle scents.

02 — Where It Stands Now

The Current State of EcoNow

This is the part you actually need to sit with.

EcoNow has lost its original focus. That's not just my opinion — it's Dr Roy's, and I agree with it. We've drifted toward profiting instead of educating and equipping the children with skills, which was the entire point of starting this in the first place.

But it's worth saying: EcoNow's mission hasn't drifted for no reason. It's run into real constraints and ceilings — the kind that push a student club toward "just sell more product" because that's the path of least resistance. I'll get into those next.

The organizer deviated from the original idea of the competition — supporting university students with Social Entrepreneurship — and instead pushed students to register and start an independent Social Enterprise through SSM and related government bodies. — According to Dr Roy, on how MOHE's framing of the UCSE competition changed

You can trace the goal misorientation back to EcoNow's participation in the UCSE competition, run by the Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE), which granted us RM 40,000. Dr Roy strongly disagrees with the idea of formally registering and running a social enterprise off the back of that — because we're a student-run club. We don't have the time, manpower, or operational capability to run a consistently profitable enterprise on top of everything else.

03 — The Fundamentals

The Ceiling of EcoNow

Some things just wouldn't work. Before you become the OM, you need to know why.

Before you take over as Operational Manager, understand this: EcoNow has hit a ceiling, and it needs restructuring. Let me show you the math first — it explains more than any conversation could.

RM 1,728 sounds fine until you remember rental, transport, and R&D still have to come out of it. That's the ceiling, in numbers. Now here's the ceiling in practice.

Constraint 1 — Production

  • Can our beneficiaries even produce every single month? Things get repetitive fast — and at that point, where's the educational value we're supposed to be giving them?
  • Production sessions with beneficiaries can get chaotic and need tight coordination. The candles that come out of it aren't always up to standard.
  • Beneficiaries can't handle complex candle formulas — some are already struggling with basic math.
  • Every production session takes real time: clean-up, teaching, coordination, transport. Doing this even once a month requires serious member dedication.
