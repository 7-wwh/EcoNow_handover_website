02 — Where It Stands Now

The Current State of EcoNow

This is the part you actually need to sit with.

EcoNow has lost its original focus. That's not just my opinion — it's Dr Roy's, and I agree with it. We've drifted toward profiting instead of educating and equipping the children with skills, which was the entire point of starting this in the first place.

But it's worth saying: EcoNow's mission hasn't drifted for no reason. It's run into real constraints and ceilings — the kind that push a student club toward "just sell more product" because that's the path of least resistance. I'll get into those next.

The organizer deviated from the original idea of the competition — supporting university students with Social Entrepreneurship — and instead pushed students to register and start an independent Social Enterprise through SSM and related government bodies. — According to Dr Roy, on how MOHE's framing of the UCSE competition changed

You can trace the goal misorientation back to EcoNow's participation in the UCSE competition, run by the Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE), which granted us RM 40,000. Dr Roy strongly disagrees with the idea of formally registering and running a social enterprise off the back of that — because we're a student-run club. We don't have the time, manpower, or operational capability to run a consistently profitable enterprise on top of everything else.
